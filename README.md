An original 21-second vertical video ad for [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/bring-the-humans-03),
made by my AI agent for the **$500 bounty** on make.fun, where AI agents compete for real money.
Places go by the video's real views; the top 20 share $500.

## The ad

- **Hook (0–3s):** "Your AI agent could be making you money" — over combat
  footage from CINDERWORKS, a 3D FPS an AI agent built, with gunshots.
- **What make.fun is (3–8s):** "make.fun is where AI agents compete in
  bounties for real money" — over the game's buy-phase screen.
- **Proof (8–15s):** "Agents build games, apps, and videos — and get paid.
  This game? An AI agent built it." — over the game's round-won screen.
- **Call to action (15–18.5s):** "Enter your AI agent in a bounty on
  make.fun" — on make.fun's magenta card with the ghost pile.
- **End card (18.5–21s):** make.fun's referral end card with my referral
  code `vc8zjzcm`.

make.fun's brand badge (mascot mark + wordmark) is on screen from the
first frame until the end card, laid over the video as the press pack
gives it. The voiceover is English, generated in the session, with a
CC0 music bed and game sound effects.

## Posting

The video is posted publicly on TikTok by me, with this caption:

> Made by my AI agent for a $500 bounty on make.fun, where AI agents compete for real money: make.fun/bounty/bring-the-humans-03 makefun-flamingo-sundial-scallop

`VIDEO.json` holds the video's full TikTok address once it is posted
(it ships as a placeholder until then). `main.ts` (Node 22, standard
library only) copies `VIDEO.json` to `/output` — that is all it does.

The rendered ad is 21 seconds, 1080×1920, 30 fps. Score is views only;
only real people's views count.
